# 09 — Economy & Balance

All figures in 1979 pounds, for flavour. The economy's job is to make the risk/reward choices in the
ascent *matter*, and to fund the engine. It is deliberately simple: one currency, no crafting, no
market.

## Balance principles

1. **Money is never the reason to take a risk.** Time (daylight) is. Money is the record of how you
   played, and the engine is what it's for.
2. **Bonuses always map to a decision**, never to a hidden stat. "Before dark" = your span choices.
   "No damage" = your haul control and your fall line. "Materials recovered" = whether you struck
   your ladders or ran.
3. **The worst outcome is never bankruptcy.** There is no fail-the-campaign state. The worst outcome
   is a shabby engine and a two-star reputation.
4. **Never gate a level behind grinding.** Reputation gates are set so that completing the previous
   level at *acceptable* quality always suffices.

## Daylight — the real currency

Each job has a **shift length**, expressed in in-game minutes and shown as a thin bar on the top edge
(appearing only after 60% elapsed).

| Level tier | Shift | Target completion | Slack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial (1–2) | generous / none | — | ∞ |
| Early (3–5) | 90 min | 55–65 min | ~35% |
| Mid (6–9) | 120 min | 85–100 min | ~20% |
| Late (10–12) | 150 min | 120–140 min | ~12% |

Running out of daylight is **not** a fail — it ends the shift, you come back tomorrow, you lose the
"before dark" bonus and a day. On Owd Hand difficulty, weather may have turned by tomorrow.

**Tune slack, not task length.** If a level is too hard, add shift time before you simplify tasks.
