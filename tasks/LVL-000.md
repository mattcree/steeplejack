## Goal
The 55 m grey-box chimney the MVP is tested on: four bands, no mission, open field.

## Why
MVP criterion 2 is 'is the loop still interesting at anchor 12'. That question is only askable if the level has the band variety the Ascent Beat Rule demands.

## Context
Four bands over 55 m: plain (0-16), salt-bloom (16-30, where visual reading fails and tapping is the only option), old-fixtures (30-42, the speed-for-uncertainty gamble), perished top (42-55, short spans when the player wants to hurry). Archetype `SURVEY` with zero defects — the mission system does not exist yet and the level must load without one.

## Acceptance
1. `make validate` passes with no warnings.
2. No `plain` band exceeds 20 m.
3. `loadoutHint.ladders` is 14, forcing a ~3.9 m average span — comfortable, so span risk is a choice rather than a requirement.
4. The four bands produce four visibly and audibly different climbing experiences when played.

## Out of scope
No mission, no exclusions, no weather ramp, no scoring bonuses. This level exists to be climbed.

### Decisions
1. **`order: 99` is a sentinel, not a position.** This level is not in the campaign sequence, but
   the schema requires `order` >= 1 with no "unordered" value. 99 was the least misleading option.
   Follow-up worth raising: let `order` be `0` or `null` for non-campaign levels.
2. **Band boundaries 16 / 30 / 42 / 55**, taken from the task's Context. Gives spans of 16, 14, 12
   and 13 m — inside the Ascent Beat Rule's 15–20 m cadence throughout, and the `plain` band at
   16 m sits under the 20 m ceiling with room to spare.
3. **Scoring is deliberately inert** (`bonuses: []`). MVP criterion 2 asks whether the *climb*
   holds attention; a bonus structure would supply a competing reason to continue and contaminate
   the answer. The task's "no scoring bonuses" says the same thing.
4. **`shiftMinutes: null`** — no clock. Same reasoning: time pressure would confound the question
   being asked. `targetMinutes: 25` is left as a non-binding reference.
5. **Band `params` keys are provisional and labelled as such** in the design doc.
   `visualReadReliability`, `bloomCoverage`, `fixtureCount`, `fixtureSpacingMetres`,
   `fixtureRated`, `soundJointClustering` express intent. The generators in
   `SteeplejackSim/Private/Joints.cpp` do not exist yet, so these are a proposal for whoever
   writes them, not a settled interface. Flagged rather than guessed silently because band
   behaviour is game design.

### Surprises
- **The reachability check is tighter than it looks.** 14 ladders x 4.0 m comfortable = 56 m
  against 55 m climbed — one metre of slack. At 13 ladders the validator would warn. The task's
  "14 ladders, ~3.9 m average, comfortable" is exactly right, but it is right by 1 m, not by a
  margin. Worth knowing before anyone edits the height.
- **The content-status table in `level-index.md` is stale** for rows I did not own: it shows Data
  as ⬜ for L1–L4 and L5–L7, but `01-back-yard.json` and `06-waterside.json` both exist. I did not
