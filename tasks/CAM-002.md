## Goal
Three set-piece camera modes: looking down the haul rope, the wide fall, and the top-of-chimney reveal.

## Why
The haul shot is the money shot and it is free, because the player is standing still. The top camera is what MVP criterion 1 is measuring.

## Context
During a haul the camera rotates to look down the rope — the player is static so there is no control conflict. The fall camera snaps wide with 0.6x time dilation and the stack streaking past. The top camera pulls out to 4 m, drops to eye level and widens FOV. All three must respect the accessibility motion settings.

## Acceptance
1. The haul camera looks down the rope and holds the load and the ground in frame.
2. The fall camera keeps the character centred and the stack visible.
3. Reaching the top triggers the wide reveal once, and only once, per ascent.
4. All three respect motion accessibility settings, including 'minimal' fall camera.
5. No mode ever takes control away mid-verb.

## Out of scope
The tea camera is METER-004. Survey and gob cameras are M4.
