## Goal
Ground ambience recedes, wind rises, and reverb lengthens as the player climbs.

## Why
The single most effective and cheapest way to sell altitude. A player should be able to hear how high they are with their eyes shut — and MVP criterion 1 depends on it.

## Context
Over 0 to 120 m: ground ambience -18 dB with a low-pass down to 2.5 kHz and reverb wet to 0.35; wind from -40 dB to 0 dB; your own sounds unchanged but with a longer reverb tail; sparrows give way to swifts and jackdaws above 40 m; spatial width narrows. The ground bed is layered and keyed to the town: a dog, a train, children, a mill hooter, a bus.

## Acceptance
1. A blindfolded tester estimates height within 20 m at five sample heights.
2. Mechanical-signal sounds (tap, gust) duck everything else by 6 dB and remain audible at 55 m.
3. A 'clarity' accessibility mix option boosts signals a further 6 dB and lowers ambience.
4. No audible stepping as height changes — all parameters are continuous.
5. Bus layout is data, not code.

## Out of scope
No music (M3). No binaural or 7.1 work (M4, for the fall).
