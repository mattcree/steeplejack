## Goal
A hammer swing with anticipation, a real arc, a hard impact frame and a two-frame hold on contact.

## Why
The most-repeated action in the game. The GDD says 'spend a week on the hammer' and means it — this single animation carries more of the game's feel than anything except the climbing IK.

## Context
Requires the Unreal editor (animation tree), so it is batched with other editor work. The draw phase must be poseable continuously from the power value, not a fixed-length clip: the player holds to draw and releases when they choose.

## Acceptance
1. Draw pose is driven continuously by power in [0,1].
2. Impact has a two-frame hold and a screen-space impulse of 2-3 px.
3. Follow-through exists; the hammer does not snap back to idle.
4. The swing reads clearly from the work camera at 1.4 m.
5. A dust puff spawns on the impact frame, not before or after.

## Out of scope
No audio (AUD-002). No IK (CLIMB-005). No variations for tool condition.
