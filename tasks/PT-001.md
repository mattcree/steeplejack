## Goal
Answer the project's central question: is climbing fun on its own?

## Why
This is the only gate that can kill the project, and it should be allowed to. Nothing from M2 onward starts before it passes.

## Context
Grey box, 55 m, no mission, headphones mandatory, one silent facilitator. The script is: 'This is a game about a steeplejack. Get to the top. I'm not going to help you.' Record screen, face and hands. Ship a replay from every session. The most important data is the answer to survey question 2 — if people are thinking about brickwork, spans and weather, the game works; if they are thinking about buttons, it does not.

## Acceptance
1. Eight participants, none of whom have seen the game.
2. All seven MVP criteria scored numerically in the write-up.
3. Every fall has a recorded player explanation.
4. Replays captured for all eight sessions.
5. The write-up lists three things that surprised us and the task IDs created.
6. A go / no-go / fix-and-retest recommendation is stated explicitly.

## Out of scope
Do not fix anything during the test window. Collect, then decide.
