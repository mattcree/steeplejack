## Goal
Wind that scales with height, and gusts that always announce themselves 1.2 seconds in advance.

## Why
The gust tell is one of the game's real skills and one of its clearest fairness commitments. A gust with no tell is a bug, not a difficulty.

## Context
`gust_incoming()` is a promise: once it returns a value below the pre-roll time, the gust **will** happen. Never surprise the player. The audio is a rushing sound approaching across the rooftops; the visual fallback is screen-edge wind streaks. Both ship in this task.

## Interface
```cpp
class_name Weather extends RefCounted
func step(dt: float, shift_fraction: float) -> void
func wind_at(height: float) -> float
func gust_now() -> float
func gust_incoming() -> float
func precipitation() -> String
```

## Acceptance
1. Wind follows the level's `windAtHeight` curve.
2. Every gust is preceded by `gust_incoming()` counting down through 1.2 s — asserted over 1,000 simulated gusts with zero exceptions.
3. The audio pre-roll and the screen-edge streak both fire from the same signal.
4. Gust intensity feeds `Wobble.amplitude_deg` and the nerve wind factor.
5. Deterministic for a given seed.

## Out of scope
Rain, the storm ramp and wet-grip are WEATH-001 at M2.

## Outcome
**What changed:** the wind model is `Source/SteeplejackSim/Public/Wind.h` and
`Private/Wind.cpp`, not `Weather.h`/`Weather.cpp`. The tests are `tests/unit/test_wind.cpp`, not
`test_weather.cpp`. Those are the declared deviations from `owns`. The model reads the level's
`weather` block, which is `WeatherSpec` in `Level.h`. It runs gusts through Calm → Tell → Rise →
Hold → Fall, and gust strength is exactly zero for the whole of the 1.2 s tell. The Unreal
`WindVisuals.cpp` is replaced under ADR-0006 by the HUD streak in `godot/scripts/hud.gd`
(`_draw_gust_tell`) and the `gustTell` cue in `godot/scripts/foley.gd`.

Acceptance:

1. "Wind: speed follows the authored height curve" covers the height curve.
2. "Wind: nothing bites without a tell in front of it" runs until it has seen 1,000 gusts, and
   asserts none bit without a full pre-roll.
3. The audio cue (`player.gd`, on the rising edge of `jack.gust_tell()`) and the HUD streak
   (`hud.gd`, while `jack.gust_tell()`) read the same sim flag. Nothing is on a timer of its own.
4. `jack.wobble_deg(jack.gust_strength())`, and `set_context` passes the gusting wind at the
   player's height, which feeds the nerve wind factor.
5. "Wind: the same seed gusts at the same moments" covers determinism.

**Decisions made:** a null gust interval in a level means a sheltered level, not a gust every
frame, and there is a test for it. A gust multiplies the wind it is gusting over
(`gustWindMultiplier`) instead of adding a fixed amount, so a gust on a calm day is small.

**Surprises:** the only caller of `wobble_deg` passed a gust of zero, so gusts could not move your
hands. Fixed in the binding's caller.

**Follow-ups:** none in scope.
