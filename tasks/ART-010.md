## Goal
One layered master material for fired brick and lime mortar, parameterised so that the four joint
quality tiers are **visually distinguishable at 2 m without sound**.

## Why
**This is the single most important asset in the project.** It is simultaneously the look and the
gameplay read. Under the previous art direction one procedural shader had to carry the joint tiers
alone; the whole point of moving to Unreal was that scanned materials make this read legible.

Pulled forward from M3 to M1 by ADR-0004, because it is now a gameplay dependency of the MVP test,
not a polish pass.

## Context
Megascans fired brick plus lime mortar, blended. Per-instance parameters, driven from the level
JSON `weathering` block and from per-joint quality:

| Parameter | Drives |
|---|---|
| `SootGradient` | vertical soot falloff — dirtiest at the base |
| `BleachHeight` | sun-bleaching toward the top |
| `MortarErosion` | **sound vs. fair vs. perished** — recessed, sandy, pitted |
| `SaltBloom` | white efflorescence — mortar that *looks* perished and is not |
| `CrackMask` | hairline fractures through the brick — the Cracked tier |
| `WetnessLerp` | rain levels |

Parallax occlusion or real displacement on the mortar joints is what makes the erosion read at 2 m.
Budget 4K virtual textures here and 2K everywhere else.

**Risk and fallback.** Four days is the estimate for getting the tier read working, not for a
finished material. If the blind sort test is still failing at day 4, the fallback is to add a
subtle non-photoreal cue — a very slight desaturation and roughness shift per tier, applied
uniformly — which is less beautiful and unambiguously readable. Take it rather than overrunning;
the gameplay read is the requirement, the beauty is the bonus.

**The hard part is `SaltBloom` vs. `MortarErosion`.** They must look similar at a glance and
different on inspection, because the salt-bloom band (grey-box level, and Level 9) exists precisely
to defeat a careless visual read and force a tap test. Get that relationship wrong and a whole
band type stops working.

## Acceptance
1. Blind sort test: 8 of 8 testers correctly order screenshots of the four tiers at 2 m, no audio.
2. Salt bloom is mistaken for perished mortar at 5 m by a majority, and correctly distinguished at
   1 m by a majority. **Both halves must hold.**
3. All six parameters are per-instance and driven from level JSON without a material recompile.
4. One master material, instances only — no per-level material duplication.
5. Within the texture streaming budget at 4K virtual textures.
6. Reads correctly under all four sky palette values and in rain.

## Out of scope
Stone, concrete and steel get their own instances later (STRUCT-003, CONC-001). Not this task.
