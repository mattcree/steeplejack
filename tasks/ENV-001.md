## Goal
Layered volumetric fog with a controllable inversion layer, plus per-level and per-weather density,
driven from data.

## Why
Atmospheric perspective is the second strongest height cue after ground parallax, and in this game
it is a **gameplay system**, not a garnish — it is how the player reads their own altitude.

## Context
The break-out-of-the-fog-into-sunlight moment is a designed set-piece for two later levels, so build
the inversion layer as a parameter now even though the grey-box level does not use it.

Volumetric fog is budgeted at 2.0 ms and Lumen at 4.0 ms; that is 36% of the frame and it is the
thing the engine change was bought for. **Tune fog scattering distribution before touching Lumen
quality** if you need to claw back time.

## Acceptance
1. The town visibly desaturates and blues with distance.
2. An inversion layer parameter exists and can be positioned at an arbitrary height.
3. Fog density is per-level and per-weather, read from the level JSON.
4. Volumetric fog stays within 2.0 ms and Lumen within 4.0 ms on the primary target.
5. One directional light plus a sky light; no additional shadow casters.
6. Works at all four sky palette values from the art direction.

## Out of scope
Weather particles are ENV-003. The lighting baseline itself is ENV-010.
