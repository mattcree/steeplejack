## Goal
Exponential height fog plus distance fog, tuned so the town goes blue with distance and an inversion layer can be climbed through.

## Why
Atmospheric perspective is the second strongest height cue we have, after ground parallax. Without it the MVP test answers the wrong question.

## Context
Height fog in the material, not a volumetric pass (perf budget). The break-out-of-the-fog-into-sunlight moment is a designed set-piece for two later levels, so build the inversion layer as a parameter now even though the grey-box level does not use it.

## Acceptance
1. The town visibly desaturates and blues with distance.
2. An inversion layer parameter exists and can be positioned at an arbitrary height.
3. Under 1.5 ms of the frame budget.
4. One directional light and one ambient gradient, per the art direction.
5. Fog density is per-level and per-weather, driven from data.

## Out of scope
No volumetric lighting, no god rays, no weather particles (ENV-003).
