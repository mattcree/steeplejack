## Goal
An instanced block-silhouette mill town that reads correctly from 0 m and from 55 m.

## Why
Half of the MVP question is 'is the height impressive'. Grey box with no world below would answer it dishonestly — the test would be invalid.

## Context
Grey box quality is fine; this is about parallax and scale, not detail. What matters is small moving things at ground level (a bus, a canal boat, washing lines) because parallax against ground detail is the strongest visual height cue we have. Six to eight kit pieces is enough for M1; the full 40-piece kit is ART-012 at M3.

## Acceptance
1. Under 12 draw calls and 250k triangles.
2. At least two small moving elements are visible from 55 m.
3. Readable as a town from ground level and as a pattern from the top.
4. Uses only palette members from the art direction doc.
5. Placed procedurally from a seed so it is not a hand-built scene.

## Out of scope
No unique textures, no interiors, no full kit. ART-012 replaces this at M3.
