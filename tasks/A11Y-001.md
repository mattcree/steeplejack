## Goal
The motion options table, with camera sway and head bob defaulting to off.

## Why
The game is about being very high up and that will make some people genuinely unwell. Defaults matter more than availability.

## Context
Camera sway (the nerve effect) and head bob default to **off**. This is a deliberate choice: the nerve sway is a great effect and it excludes people, so it is opt-in. The rest — FOV, screen shake, time dilation, reduce-look-down, minimal fall camera — are all sliders or toggles.

## Acceptance
1. Every row of the motion table exists and works.
2. Camera sway and head bob default to off.
3. 'Reduce look-down' clamps pitch to -60 deg and softens ground parallax.
4. Settings persist and apply without a restart.
5. The grey-box level is completable with every motion option off.
6. No flashing above 3 Hz anywhere, verified.

## Out of scope
Audio, motor and cognitive options are A11Y-002 at M2.
