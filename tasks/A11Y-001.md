## Goal
The motion options table, with camera sway and head bob defaulting to off.

## Why
The game is about being very high up and that will make some people genuinely unwell. Defaults matter more than availability.

## Context
Camera sway (the nerve effect) and head bob default to **off**. This is a deliberate choice: the nerve sway is a great effect and it excludes people, so it is opt-in. The rest — FOV, screen shake, time dilation, reduce-look-down, minimal fall camera — are all sliders or toggles.

## Acceptance
1. Every row of the motion table exists and works.
2. Camera sway and head bob default to off.
3. 'Reduce look-down' clamps pitch to -60 deg and softens ground parallax.
4. Settings persist and apply without a restart.
5. The grey-box level is completable with every motion option off.
6. No flashing above 3 Hz anywhere, verified.

## Out of scope
Audio, motor and cognitive options are A11Y-002 at M2.

## Outcome
**What changed:** under ADR-0006 this is GDScript, not `MotionOptions.cpp`/`Settings.h`.
Settings do not decide anything in the sim, so none of it belongs in `SteeplejackSim`. The files
are:

- `godot/scripts/settings.gd` (`GameSettings`): the eight rows of the motion table, kept in
  `user://settings.cfg` and applied through a `changed` signal.
- An F1 overlay drawn in `hud.gd`, with input in `player.gd`: arrows to choose and change,
  keyboard only.
- `godot/scripts/test_options.gd`, which is in `make godot-test`.

Acceptance:

1. Every row exists, and each one does something. Three rows had nothing to switch until now:
   - Camera sway: a slow roll of up to 1.5° with nerve, on a ten-second cycle.
   - Head bob: a couple of centimetres at a run, on the ground only.
   - Vignette on low nerve: a steady radial tunnel in the bottom two bands. It never pulses.

   FOV (60–100°), screen shake (0–100%, scaling the hammer kick) and the two fall options were
   rewired off the old `motion_minimal` flag. Time dilation off gives an immediate cut. The minimal
   fall camera gives an immediate cut with the view held still.
2. Sway and head bob default to off (tested).
3. Reduce look-down clamps pitch to −60°, both at once and against the mouse (tested). **"Softens
   ground parallax" is not done:** there is no parallax layer in this build to soften. The clamp
   is the part that stops the player staring straight down.
4. Settings persist through a scratch file round trip and apply without a restart (tested). A
   hand-edited file with the wrong type for a row is ignored for that row, so a typo cannot switch
   sway on.
5. `test_ascent` climbs the Grey Box to the top with the defaults. None of the options changes a
   rule, only the camera and the HUD, so every option off plays the same. The task's own verify
   line is a human playthrough, and that has not been done.
6. Every oscillation in the HUD and the world was read: the fastest is the buckle warning at
   (8 + 10) rad/s = 2.9 Hz, just under the 3 Hz limit. The brazier's light is static, and the fall
   is fades. **Nothing measures this automatically**, so a new pulse could break it silently. A
   convention check on `sin(t * k)` with k > 6π would be cheap.

**Decisions made:** tests and shots never read the player's `settings.cfg`. Only the running main
scene does, so someone's vertigo settings cannot make a test pass or fail. The overlay does not
pause, because the climb has no pause. Open it on the ground or belted on.

**Follow-ups:** the 3 Hz convention check, and ground parallax if a parallax layer is ever added.
