## Goal
A camera rig with the climb, work and top modes, blending between them, never clipping the structure.

## Why
Height is sold by the camera as much as by the fog. The top-of-chimney framing is directly load-bearing for MVP criterion 1.

## Context
Over-shoulder 2.2 m back and 15 deg above for climbing; pulls to 1.4 m and focuses the hands for work; pulls out to 4 m and drops to eye level at the top. Rule: never cut through the chimney, always push out. The horizon must stay visible above 20 m — that is the primary height cue.

## Acceptance
1. No camera position at any player height or yaw puts geometry between the camera and the player.
2. Mode transitions blend over 0.3-0.5 s and never during an active verb.
3. The horizon is visible at every pitch angle when the player is above 20 m.
4. FOV is driven by a setting, default 75, range 60-100.
5. A headless test sweeps 360 deg of yaw at 5 heights and asserts no occlusion.

## Out of scope
Haul, fall and tea cameras are CAM-002 and METER-004. Motion accessibility options are A11Y-001.
