## Goal
The project's lighting rig: Lumen GI and reflections, one directional sun, a sky light, and a
two-key daylight ramp across the shift.

## Why
Roughly 80% of why the key art works is lighting and atmosphere rather than material fidelity. This
is where "big graphics" actually comes from, and it is cheap relative to its impact.

## Context
Everything downstream — ART-010's material look-dev, ENV-001's fog, ENV-002's town — needs a
lighting baseline to be authored against, so this lands early and other look-dev tasks depend on it.

The industrial North is an overcast-or-golden-hour place; both are single-source. Resist adding
fill lights — the bounce from Lumen off a brick face is what should be lighting the character's
underside, and that is the thing that makes the image feel photographed.

Time of day is a **two-key lerp across the shift**, not a full cycle. Levels pick their two keys.

## Acceptance
1. Lumen GI and reflections enabled and within the 4.0 ms budget on the primary target.
2. One directional light plus a sky light; no other shadow casters.
3. A two-key daylight ramp driven by shift fraction, configured per level from JSON.
4. Reference scene renders correctly at overcast, golden hour and dusk.
5. Sky colours match the four palette values in the art direction.
6. No light leaking or Lumen flicker on the chimney interior (the flue) — check this specifically,
   it is a narrow dark shaft and a known Lumen weak spot.

## Out of scope
Volumetric fog is ENV-001. Weather is ENV-003.
