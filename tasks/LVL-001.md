## Goal
Somebody climbs `00-greybox` from 0 to 55 m and answers whether the four bands actually feel
like four different things.

## Why
This is **LVL-000's acceptance criterion 4**, which could not be checked when the level was
authored because nothing was playable. It was deferred honestly rather than self-certified, and
this task exists so that deferral does not quietly evaporate — an unfiled criterion is a
criterion nobody ever checks.

It matters more than it sounds. MVP criterion 2 is "is the loop still interesting at anchor 12",
and the only reason to believe the answer is yes is band variety. If the four bands read as one
band, the level cannot answer the question the whole MVP is built to ask.

## Context
The level is four bands over 55 m — `plain` (0–16), `salt-bloom` (16–30), `old-fixtures`
(30–42), `perished` (42–55). Their intent is written up in
[`level-00-greybox.md`](../docs/02-levels/level-00-greybox.md), including which beat each band
is supposed to deliver and which is the hero beat (first tap into salt bloom at ~17 m).

Judge what the player experiences, not what the JSON says. Specifically:

- **Does the salt-bloom band actually defeat visual reading?** If a player can still eyeball
  joints through the bloom, the band has no function and the tap-test verb has not been proven.
- **Is the old-fixtures gamble a real decision?** If taking a free unrated dog is obviously
  correct or obviously foolish, it is not a gamble, it is a tax.
- **Does the perished top actually shorten spans?** This depends on how the generator clusters
  sound joints, which was unknown when the level was written. It may sail through.

Three open questions in the design doc bear on this — read them first; they are the same
doubts, written down before anyone could test them.

## Interface
No code. Findings go into the design doc.

## Acceptance
1. The level is climbed 0 to 55 m at least twice, once by someone who did not author it.
2. Each of the four bands is judged distinguishable or not, in writing, with the reason.
3. `docs/02-levels/level-00-greybox.md` "Open questions" is updated — resolved entries deleted,
   unresolved ones sharpened. Do not leave stale entries.
4. If a band does not read as distinct, a follow-up task is raised against the band or the
   generator. Do not silently retune `00-greybox.json` — the data may not be what is wrong.

## Out of scope
No retuning of the level in this task, and no changes to `data/levels/00-greybox.json`. This is
a judgement, not a fix; the fix is whatever task this raises.
