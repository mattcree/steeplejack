## Goal
Four recorded tap sounds — sound, fair, perished, cracked — that are distinguishable by ear under bad conditions.

## Why
The tap test is the game's signature action and its single most-repeated sound. If the four are not distinguishable, the core 'read the material' skill does not exist and MVP criterion 5 fails.

## Context
Design on the attack and decay envelope, not timbre: sound = sharp 2 ms attack / 120 ms decay; fair = 8 ms / 200 ms; perished = 20 ms / 400 ms low-passed at 800 Hz; cracked = sharp plus a secondary transient at 40 ms with an inharmonic rattle. Record for real — a hammer, a brick, a bag of sand and a cracked paving slab beats forty hours of synthesis. Four to six variations each to avoid machine-gunning.

## Acceptance
1. Four banks exist with at least 4 variations each.
2. The listening test passes: 8/8 on laptop speakers in mono at low volume.
3. Each sound is under 500 ms and has a real transient that survives the height mix's low-pass.
4. Peak levels are matched so tier is conveyed by envelope, not loudness.
5. Files are OGG, mono, 48 kHz.

## Out of scope
No implementation — VERB-001 wires them up. No hammer impact sounds — that is AUD-002.
