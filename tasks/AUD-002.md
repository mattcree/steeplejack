## Goal
Impact sounds per joint tier, wired to the strike result, with a transient that survives the height mix.

## Why
The strike is how the player feels the material. A dull thud on soft mortar and a ring on sound mortar is the same information the tap test gives, confirmed by touch.

## Context
Four tiers x at least four variations, plus a distinct bent-dog sound and a distinct spall sound. The bent-dog sound must be unmistakable — it costs the player material and they need to know immediately.

## Acceptance
1. Four tier banks plus bent-dog and spall, four variations each.
2. The transient is audible over wind at 55 m, verified through AUD-003's mix.
3. Sound selection is driven by `StrikeResult`, not by the animation.
4. Bent dog is distinguishable from a normal strike in a blind test.
5. No sample repeats audibly within a five-strike sequence.

## Out of scope
No tap sounds (AUD-001). No mixing rules (AUD-003).
