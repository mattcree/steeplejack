## Goal
The minimal HUD: two meter arcs, in-world anchor rating pips, material counts, nothing else.

## Why
The fairness contract requires the player always knows what they are hanging from. MVP criterion 3 — can every player explain why they fell — is measured directly against this.

## Context
Anchor pips are in-world, not screen-space, so the player reads the stack by looking at it. Meters fade out above 85 and when idle. No minimap, no objective marker — the objective is at the top and you can see it. Ratings use shape plus text, never colour alone.

## Acceptance
1. Grip arc is fast and responsive; nerve arc visibly breathes with the character's breathing.
2. Both fade out above 85 and when idle.
3. Anchor pips render in-world at every anchor, legible at 20 m, using shape and not colour alone.
4. The cascade animation burns pips bottom-of-stack upward in failure order.
5. Readable at 720p and at 200% UI scale.
6. Fully legible in greyscale and in all three colour-blind modes.

## Out of scope
No reckoning screen (M2), no daylight bar (M2), no tool wheel (M2).
