## Goal
The foley beds: rope under tension, ladder creak and flex, boots on rungs, breathing at four nerve levels.

## Why
The ascent has no music by design. Foley is the entire soundscape of the game's longest activity.

## Context
Breathing is tied to the nerve meter and is one of the two ways low nerve is communicated without UI (the other is the character's body language). Four intensities, cross-faded, not switched.

## Acceptance
1. Banks exist for: rope tension, rope running, ladder creak, ladder flex under load, boot on rung, boot slip, breathing x4 intensities, cloth movement.
2. Each bank has enough variations that 30 seconds of climbing never repeats a sample audibly.
3. Breathing layers cross-fade without a seam.
4. Everything is mono and spatialised at runtime, not pre-panned.

## Out of scope
No mixing or height falloff — that is AUD-003.
